DiPlay Android 4.3 — 0.16 experimental
Reference: upstream 0.2.8 / f2d06951. Implementation status and hardware acceptance are separate.

Previously confirmed on this head unit:
• Wireless CarPlay through the existing hotspot; full-screen H.264 display and touch.
• Music, navigation synchronization/announcements and Siri invocation.
• Steering voice button callback; independent head-unit GPS fixes.
• Three diagnostic tones on the Android music/call/notification streams.
Baidu navigation behavior has been accepted; no further navigation investigation is requested.

Newly integrated in 0.16, awaiting hardware acceptance:
• Wired USBMUX/Lockdown/carkit/iAP2 and NCM/IPv6 VPN path.
• Saved hotspot, app-managed WPA2 hotspot, Wi-Fi Direct group owner and shared WPA2 router modes.
• Upstream audio channel mapping, SDK18 audio focus, app volume and 300/500/1000 ms media buffer.
• Optional navigation music ducking; OFF by default, preserving upstream overlay behavior.
• Configurable resolution, 30–60 FPS, upstream 0.3x–1.0x display scale, physical dimensions and basis, independent safe-area edges, outside-safe-area drawing and right-hand drive.
• Foreground wireless session service; Surface reattachment, background audio/GPS, disconnect retry with bounded backoff and optional boot connection.
• OEM CarPlay return icon and Android launcher return.
• Steering short press opens CarPlay; long press invokes Siri (MCU221 or timed window long press). New split needs real verification: earlier logs only contain MCU93. If both physical presses report 93 without timing, they cannot be distinguished by this callback.
• Native API18 settings using original English/Chinese/Arabic/Spanish/Russian strings and system-language selection.

Capability-dependent, not guaranteed on this firmware:
• HEVC decoder and Opus decoder/encoder paths are implemented. The app only advertises them when enabled AND the platform codecs exist. The supplied firmware has no verified HEVC/Opus codecs; there is no bundled software codec.
• USB Host, CDC-NCM, IPv6 and TUN are enabled in the supplied firmware. That does not prove the installed firmware, physical USB wiring or the complete wired session. Use the car's single USB port; no physical-port switch is required.
• USB mode may require system USB/VPN permission and iPhone Trust/CarPlay approval.
• Wi-Fi Direct/managed hotspot behavior and radio band depend on the vendor driver; SDK18 cannot reproduce newer LocalOnlyHotspot/band controls.
• Compose/AndroidX visuals are reimplemented with native widgets; exact original appearance is not claimed.

Still unverified or outside this merge:
• Reliable microphone uplink / Siri recognition, bidirectional calls, iPhone use of vehicle GPS and phone Live Activities.
• ABS/CAN vehicle speed: read-only CarinfoService integration; valid speed feeds RMC. Original PASCD is gated by a verified P/R/N/D map and phone subscription. Rear-wheel pulse counters found in lower-level firmware; conversion not implemented. Battery and instrument/HUD integration remain unavailable.
• iOS 27 parked video: cannot validate against the user's iOS 16.6.1.
• Force-stopped apps and vendor process killing cannot be bypassed by a foreground service.

Testing:
Keep the working hotspot first; verify wireless music/Siri, return to car launcher and reopen, then reconnect and optional boot-connect. Test USB and other network modes separately. Settings take effect on reconnect. Export includes Activity, background session and wired-stage logs; it does not store microphone recordings, navigation coordinates, router passwords, pairing keys or phone transcripts.

Vehicle-speed diagnostics: Menu → Diagnostics → CAN speed and raw gear (60 seconds). Configure only verified P/R/N/D codes, then reconnect. Firmware reads and phone adoption require hardware tests.
